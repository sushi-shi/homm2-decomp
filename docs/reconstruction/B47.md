# B47: redraw-grid negative indices and partial clearing

The real `bComboDraw` global begins at `0x00523e50` and is declared as an
18×18-byte array. `ComboDraw`, RVA `0xf30a`, uses the actual18-byte row
stride while drawing a15×15 viewport. All4788 function bytes, complete DIR32
sites and ordered targets independently match genuine retail:
`build/b47-combo-retail.log` (`check-function.py SOURCE/ADVMGR 0xf30a`).

Two hazards are therefore present in the retail code:

- Its `memset` requests256 bytes, not the array's324 bytes. Even viewport
  cells `[14][4]` through `[14][14]` lie outside that cleared prefix. Later
  increment/test operations can observe previous contents.
- A monster at screen column0 increments `bComboDraw[-1][drawY]`.
  Hero/boat processing at column1 and row>=1 also increments
  `bComboDraw[-1][drawY-1]`: its enclosing guard is `drawX>=1`, while the
  index subtracts2. Neither path is justified by the separate world-coordinate
  guard. No runtime reproduction is claimed.

Representative native and retail instructions are identical:

| Function offset | Bytes | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `+0xc0` | `68 00 01 00 00` | clear256 bytes |
| `+0x25a` | `83 e9 01` | monster neighbor: subtract1 from screen X |
| `+0x25d` | `6b c9 12` | multiply by actual18-byte stride |
| `+0x263` | `8a 84 11 …` | read neighbor byte, owner `0x00523e50` |
| `+0x278` | `88 84 11 …` | write incremented byte to same owner |

Positive neighbor offsets do fit: viewport coordinates0..14 plus2 reach16,
inside18. That does not excuse negative indices or the shorter clear.
All `GetCloudLookup` calls in this body are behind valid world-coordinate
guards; that distinct lookup concern is resolved by the complete body read.

Disposition: retain and report the observed behavior. Do not fabricate a
larger aggregate or negative-index padding, shift the global owner, or change
the clear count merely to make the bounds report look closed.
