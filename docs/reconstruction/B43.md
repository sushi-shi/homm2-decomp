# B43: map-coordinate contracts are accessor-specific

`advManager::GetCell` (RVA `0x8427`) substitutes underlying cell `(0,0)`
whenever either coordinate is out of range. Its133 bytes, complete DIR32
sites and ordered targets match genuine retail (`build/b43-getcell-retail.log`).
Earlier early-call concerns in `MoveHero` and `DrawCell` therefore do not
establish an out-of-object pointer. The per-function reviews are corrected.
Clamping a lookup does not validate raw coordinates subsequently stored in a
hero or sent to visibility/network helpers; those contracts remain open.

`UpdateRadar` (RVA `0x84ac`) is different: it calls the underlying `fullMap`
accessor directly. The right-neighbor clause is outside the left-neighbor
guard. The relevant retail and native instructions are identical:

| Function offset | Bytes | Operation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `+0x472` | `75 4a` | non-flag tiles jump to right-neighbor calculation |
| `+0x486` | `7d 36` | last column also jumps to right-neighbor calculation |
| `+0x4da` | `83 c2 01` | `i + 1` |
| `+0x4dd` | `6b d2 0c` | multiply by12-byte map-cell stride |
| `+0x4e9` | `8a 54 08 09` | read right neighbor's trigger byte |

For `i=width-1`, this reads the next row's first cell when another row exists.
On the last row it reads byte9 beyond the one-past cell pointer. The path
requires a visible, non-hero cell for which preceding castle clauses are false;
no local guard excludes an ordinary empty last cell. No runtime reproduction
or proof that every shipped map reaches that state is claimed.

`check-function.py SOURCE/ADVMGR 0x84ac` confirms all3767 instruction bytes,
all absolute sites and all ordinary targets. Its only six unresolved targets
are two compiler-generated float literals. [The supplementary checker](check-advm-bounds.py)
checks all six native values against their actual retail destinations:
`16.0f` at `0x004ea43c` and `480.0f` at `0x004ea438`. It also independently
checks the entire `+0x45d..+0x4fc` decision/load span and its map-width target.
Outputs: `build/b43-radar-retail.log`, `build/advm-bounds-supplement.log`.

Disposition: retain the genuine branch/access behavior and document the
unresolved domain, rather than changing it under the guise of type/owner
reconstruction. This entry does not close B43's other per-function contracts.
