# Spell-AI cast, storage and input-domain review

All eleven physical definitions in `src/SOURCE/SPELLAI.cpp` are reconciled.
ClearEffects already has its O05 review; the other ten now have individual
ten-pattern verdicts and full-body notes in `reviews.json`. The complete
1475-line file, including its private enums, numerical macros, eleven named
float modifiers and two eleven-entry duration tables, has been read.

This adds ten function reviews and one whole-file review. KB's changed table
and every consumer were inspected; this does **not** credit the rest of KB's
large data tail as fully reviewed. X_GLOBAL.h's earlier full review is retained
with a dependency follow-up. Names are reconstruction claims, not recovered
original source identifiers.

## C60: four redundant conversions

RawEffectSpellInfluence, RVA `0x9716f`, now omits the damageMax float cast,
the two casts around integer ratios and the cast around assignment of
`factor + 0.3`. The damageMin conversion stays: it makes the addition floating
point. Removing both operand casts would perform integer addition first.
The dragon/shooter ratios still perform integer division before assignment
to float; this change does not turn them into fractional ratios.

The complete five-independent-axis product contains 32 arms, all compiled in
18.690855 seconds, input restored and no wall-time truncation. The chosen arm
is trial23 (remove maximum, keep minimum, remove all three assignment casts).
With minimum retained, both maximum choices and all eight assignment choices
produce the same 1461-byte text hash `a442d5725df0001a`. Removing minimum alone
produces 1459 bytes; removing both operands produces 1455. These are measured
parent-state results, not a universal claim about every compiler state.

The batch's embedded-table partition is 76 candidate versus 77 retail blocks,
with 75 leading exact blocks; the ordinary semantic disassembler reports
77/77. This diagnostic difference is not treated as a source CFG defect or
as proof of closure. Generic exactness is false because private numeric and
table identities are not all recognized there. Final whole-function bytes,
ordered targets and explicitly verified numeric operands supply the proof.

## C61: recover the one-byte spell domain

`giSpellInfluenceToSpell` now uses `H2_ENUM_STORAGE(SpellType, u8)` in its
definition and shared declaration, with named SpellType entries. This enables
removing `SpellType(...)` at the sole consumer, RawEffectSpellInfluence.
The target VC6 representation remains u8; the existing strict-build wrapper
provides the enum conversion without widening the table to a 32-bit enum array.

All consumers are the definition, declaration and that single read. The fifteen
influence entries plus the original unused trailing zero are still exactly:

```text
09 0b 0d 0e 12 1a 1e 1f 25 26 29 65 16 10 11 00
```

The final zero is named SPELL_FIREBALL, not removed or repurposed. Native KB
bytes, the exact retail bytes at `0x004fba68`, the consumer's DIR32 operand at
function+`0x1f` and the unchanged symbol spelling are checked. See the
[typed byte-enum pattern](../patterns/typed-byte-enum-table.md).

## B77: retail behavior and caller contracts

The following sequences are preserved, not repaired as gameplay changes.
Ordinary function bytes/sites/ordered targets are checked for DoSpellAI,
DetermineEffectOfSpell, EffectSpellCreateCreature, RawEffectSpellInfluence,
EffectSpellCure, EffectSpellResurrect and EffectSpellDamage. Private constants
in the latter six receive fixed, per-site operand checks in
`check-spellai-evidence.py`; no generic unknown-relocation exception is added.

- RawEffectSpellInfluence uses the opposing army count as its bound while
  indexing the target's own side for dragon/shooter counts. For valid positive
  counts the integer ratios are0 or1; dragon's0.28 multiplier and shield's
  clamped0.45 multiplier truncate to zero. An empty opposing count can divide
  by zero. This is not silently changed to floating division or opposing-side
  indexing. In retail COFF, IDIV at0x17eb is followed by FILD at0x17f5, then the
  named dragon modifier read at0x17fd and __ftol call at0x1804.
- EffectSpellCreateCreature multiplies the creature **type ID** by its database
  fightValue. The computed spellPower/quantity is not used in that product.
  Retail loads the type local at0x1320, scales its database index by26, reloads
  the same local at0x1326 and multiplies by fightValue at record offset2.
- DetermineEffectOfSpell passes a **hex** as EffectSpellCure's targetIndex for
  CURE. The callee instead compares it with an army slot0..19. The call at
  function+0x344 loads the hex from `[ebp-4]` before pushing it; its operand
  resolves to `0x00497879`. DISPEL passes the actual army index, and mass
  variants pass-1. No normal-game reachability claim beyond this call sequence
  is needed to distinguish the two domains.
- EffectSpellCure clears its positive sum in cure mode1 after calculating the
  healing term, before combining the score. Retail COFF0x1dbc compares mode
  with1,0x1dc2 zeros `[ebp-0x10]`, and the combination follows at0x1ddc. Sums
  initialize once per side; a mirror assignment overwrites the negative sum.
- Duration lookup is `min(effectivePower-fullQuantityFlag,10)`, with no lower
  clamp. Effective power0 and flag1 would index before the table. Valid positive
  effective power is a contract, not proven for arbitrary loaded state.
  Cancel-duration lookup is different: an unsigned byte plus a boolean and
  upper clamp10 proves its index0..10.
- DoSpellAI's mana divisor is positive for the actual default combat catalog.
  All65 attribute bytes identify precisely combat IDs0..47; their costs stay
  positive after the one applicable artifact halving (minimum1). Eligibility
  proves mana/cost>=1; the upper clamp10 bounds the eleven-entry weighting
  table. The full206-byte GetManaCost comparison and its disjoint artifact
  branches support this proof, not assumptions about a modded catalog.
- FirstArmy/FirstResurrectable have only an upper bound115. Negative starting
  hexes are not validated. Normal scanner calls start at1 or previous+1;
  each returns0 on success and1 with output-1 on exhaustion. NextPos requires
  a valid scalar pointer and representable arithmetic.
- Determine's area mode stops at hex43. Its output hex can remain unwritten
  when the score is zero/default; DoSpellAI consumes it only after a positive
  score. The input side must agree with m_currentSide.
- EffectSpellResurrect assumes successful corpse selection before forming its
  army pointer; -1 is not guarded locally. EffectSpellDamage reads initial
  targetHex before its loop guard. Unsupported non-damage spell cases leave
  that loop stationary; the normal dispatcher sends the damage family.
  Damage traversal bounds currentHex to0..115 but still trusts valid positive
  occupant side/index and chain-source occupancy.

The three two-side damage arrays are genuine, fully observed owners. Remaining
bounds require valid sides, counts<=20, creature IDs, positive HP, live owners,
valid neighbor sentinels and representable arithmetic. There is no artificial
stack aggregate or manual-varargs function in this TU. No new ordinary
out-of-object pointer expression is removed; invalid index preconditions are
reported as contracts, not concealed with padding.
