# NewMap: C34, G48, R07, S36 and B56

The clean source parent is `508022f87`. The complete 410-line NewMap body,
destination declarations and campaign-name providers were reread.
Fourteen casts are removed. Both award jumps, the coordinate/counter reuse
and three unread locals remain after complete structural experiments.

## C34: natural destination conversions

Five setup/victory assignments lose casts: the current-player mask,
human-position assignment, two color-map assignments and victory-side
threshold. Seven hero assignments lose casts: starting hero ID, two owner
assignments, two campaign-award IDs and two ordinary available-hero IDs.
Both ultimate-artifact coordinate assignments also lose their casts.
The existing destination types still perform the conversions. Faction,
artifact and personality enum construction, and floating handicap
truncation, retain their real conversions.

The player and hero assignments fit their established 0..5 and 0..53
domains, with initialized player mappings and available hero slots.
Artifact coordinates are different: the existing search can produce 134
on a 144-tile map, beyond signed i8 range. The two assignment changes
preserve the same narrowing and byte storage; they do not establish a
valid signed-coordinate domain or repair downstream signed consumers.

## G48/R07/S36: structural alternatives

The award-selection product compares the original two jumps to secondHero,
a scoped do/while(false) using break, and an explicit firstHeroSelected
flag guarding the fallback paths. The alternatives preserve the priority
of the original campaign award over the expansion award and random hero,
and leave the shared second-hero selection after the join.

The coordinate product compares the original player/nTown reuse with
separate ultimateX/ultimateY locals at function scope and inside a scoped
artifact-search phase. A third group independently retains or deletes
padNum, junkVal and heroIndex; heroIndex deletion includes its unread zero
assignment. The full 3×3×2×2×2 product has 72 arms. Only the original arm
preserves the baseline text; no combination of these changes cancels the
native differences.

With all other choices retained, the measured results are:

| Alternative | Function bytes | Frame reservation |
|---|---:|---:|
| Original |7290|0xa4|
| Scoped break join |7288|0xa4|
| Selection-flag join |7321|0xa8|
| Separate coordinates, function scope |7308|0xac|
| Separate coordinates, phase scope |7308|0xac|

The two coordinate shapes and two declaration-only deletions produce
distinct byte sequences despite their equal sizes. These are measured
negative alternatives for this parent, not proof that every future
structural recovery is impossible. No fake padding or steering is added.

## B55/B56: capacities and remaining domains

The two literal award names need 13 and 11 bytes including NUL, within the
13-byte hero name. All six Joseph names need at most 11 bytes and all six
Ivan names at most 10. `check-newmap-names.py` reads both complete retail
pointer tables and checks the actual source literals and destination size.
The name providers select entry 0 for campaign maps below 4 and entries 0..5
for maps 4..9; higher indices remain invalid.

The external map filename remains a contract: gMapName has 16 bytes, so the
copied filename must use at most 15 bytes plus NUL. This does not address
aliasing, a missing source terminator or later filename mutation.

Other B55 requirements remain: six-player mappings/count/color bijection,
town/hero/list indices, primary hero availability, terrain/skill/difficulty
domains and valid loss/victory y-1 accesses. The artifact search checks
coordinate bounds before cell access, but has no final retry cap. Its
single-human distance branch reads heroIds[0] without a local hero-count
guard. Neither these contracts nor the signed-coordinate issue are hardened.

## Experiment-runner repair

The first run stopped before compilation because the RVA-marker interval
also contains the unannotated GetCastleSlot inline helper after NewMap.
The runner now selects the first definition after the requested marker,
matching source-marker ownership; later helpers do not become candidates.
It still rejects missing definitions and ambiguous first definitions.
No game code or annotation was moved to accommodate the runner.

Five regression tests cover trailing helpers, prototypes, a missing body
before the next marker, ambiguous first definitions and a missing marker.
The focused module passes 42 tests. The full suite passes 882 tests with one
skip (`build/newmap-selftest.log`). This selection fix changes neither
source generation axes nor byte/relocation proof rules.
